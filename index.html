Burias, through our lens

Burias is a quiet island in Masbate that most people only know by name. This is how it looks from our shore: the bay at low tide, coral in the shallows, the sky on a clear night.

Skimboards, snorkels & the floating cottage

Free skimboards for cafe guests, snorkel masks to rent, and the floating cottage out on the water. Golden hour here is around 4:45–6:00 PM.

A café on the sand

BAIA sits right on Laurente Beach in San Pascual. We pull espresso, make lattes with Asin Tibuok, the heritage salt from Bohol, and cook double smash burgers and rice meals. Skimboards are free for guests, and the floating cottage is a short ride out on the water.
