Commute Guide

HOW TO GET HERE

🛵 From San Pascual Town Center / Local Ports

Take a local tricycle or motorcycle (habal-habal) heading to Barangay Laurente shoreline. Inform the driver you are heading to BAIA Cafe beachfront (Plus Code: 42PF+G8).

✈️ From Masbate City & Moises R. Espinosa Airport (MBT)

From Masbate City or the airport, travel via coastal ferry/boat routes or inter-municipality vans heading towards San Pascual and Barangay Laurente.

🅿️ Parking & Beach Access

Motorcycle and car parking spaces are available adjacent to the cafe entrance road. From the parking area, it is a 30-second walk directly onto the beach sand.
